## Description

![KnuMail](banner.png)

**KnuMail** makes the mailbox in **World of Warcraft: Forever** (1.60.1) quick and safe without turning it into a second
user interface. Open your mail, click once, done. English and Spanish.

### Features

- **Open all, with filters:** choose which mail to open (auction sold, won, expired, cancelled, outbid, other mail
  with attachments), keep a number of bag slots free, and get a summary of the gold collected.
  Alt-click for gold only.
- **Safe by default:** mail with gold from players who are not your alts, friends or guild members is skipped, so a
  stranger's gold never lands in your bags without you deciding it. Cash on delivery and GM mail are never opened
  automatically.
- **Checkboxes:** tick the mails you want and open or return just those. Shift-click selects a range, Ctrl-click selects
  everything from one sender. Your selection never leaks to another page.
- **Address book:** your alts (full names, with surnames), the last 20 recipients, friends and guild, plus
  autocomplete in the "To:" field.
- **Click shortcuts:** Shift-click a mail to take everything, Ctrl-click to return it, Alt-click an item in your bags
  to attach it.
- **Clean tooltips:** each attachment listed once, with gold and COD.

### Notes

- Written from scratch for Forever. It does not contain code from Postal or its ports.
- KnuMail never sends mail by itself: it only fills in fields for you.
- Source code and issues: https://github.com/knupson/KnuMail (to be created) — MIT license.

## Descripción (castellano)

**KnuMail** hace que el buzón de **World of Warcraft: Forever** (1.60.1) sea rápido y seguro sin convertirse en otra
interfaz:
- **Abrir todo con filtros:** elegís qué correos abrir, reservás espacios libres en las bolsas y ves un resumen del oro cobrado.
- **Casillas para abrir o devolver sólo lo marcado.**
- **Libreta de destinatarios:** tus alters con nombre completo, los recientes, tus amigos y tu hermandad.
- **Atajos de clic.**

Por seguridad, los correos con oro de desconocidos se saltean y los contra reembolso nunca se abren solos. Código propio, licencia MIT.
